# Exam seasons and study-retreat windows (US / NYC)

Research for **Beside / Body Double** — when high-stakes exams create demand for sustained, in-person focused study and multi-day retreats.  
**As of:** October 2026. Dates below cite official or primary sources; always confirm current year on the linked sites.

---

## Executive summary

- **Law** has the clearest “study leave” culture: graduates and firms treat **May–July** as bar-prep season ahead of the **July** exam; a smaller **December–February** cohort prepares for the **February** bar. New York’s **legacy UBE** runs through **February 2028**; **NextGen** starts **July 2028** in NY ([NCBE NY](https://www.ncbex.org/jurisdictions/NY), [NY BOLE press release](https://www.nybarexam.org/Press/20260108%20Final%20Announcement%20-%20NextGen%20Bar%20Exam%20Transfer%20Scores.pdf)).
- **Medicine** is less “one exam week nationwide” and more **rolling 3‑month USMLE eligibility windows** at Prometric — but schools still cluster prep around **late spring (Step 1 after M2)**, **summer/fall (Step 2 CK)**, and **residency (Step 3)** ([USMLE scheduling](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/applying-scheduling)).
- **Premed (MCAT)** is highly seasonal: testing runs **January–September**, with heavy prep **4–8 weeks before** a chosen date; NYC is a listed metro for select dates ([AAMC MCAT calendar](https://students-residents.aamc.org/register-mcat-exam/us-mcat-calendar-scheduling-deadlines-and-score-release-dates-0)).
- **Nursing (NCLEX)** peaks **April–June** and again **January** after December/May graduations; **September–December** are quieter ([NCSBN 2024/2025 stats](https://www.ncsbn.org/exams/exam-statistics-and-publications/nurse-licensure-and-nclex-exam-statistics.page)).
- **CPA**: Core sections are **year-round**; **Discipline** sections cluster in **Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct** — prep spikes in the **month before each window** ([AICPA score release / windows](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/article/find-out-when-youll-get-your-cpa-exam-score)).
- **CFA Level I**: Four windows — **Feb, May, Aug, Nov**; prep typically **8–12 weeks** before each ([CFA Institute](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/exam)).
- **Universal academic overlay:** **Finals** (~**Dec 10–20** and **May 1–15**) drive undergrad and professional-school demand even when licensing exams are rolling.
- **Best retreat strategy:** Run intensives **2–6 weeks before** exam clusters, not during exam week. Stagger offerings across **4–5 seasons** so NYC isn’t dependent on bar prep alone.

## Law

### Bar exam (UBE / NextGen)

| Administration | Typical dates (national) | Who’s studying |
|----------------|-------------------------|----------------|
| July | e.g. **Jul 28–29, 2026**; **Jul 27–28, 2027** ([NCBE NextGen dates](https://help2.ncbex.org/hc/en-us/articles/42823312209435-NextGen-UBE-Test-Dates)) | May law grads; repeaters |
| February | e.g. **Feb 23–24, 2027**; **Feb 22–23, 2028** | Repeaters; some accelerated paths |

- **Biannual** February and July on the same national dates ([NCBE FAQ](https://help2.ncbex.org/hc/en-us/articles/39896338223003-How-often-will-the-NextGen-UBE-be-administered)).
- **Commercial bar prep** (Barbri, Themis, etc.) typically runs **~8–10 weeks** full-time before July; many employers grant **study leave** for first-time takers May–July.

### New York–specific

- **Legacy UBE final administration in NY:** **February 2028** ([NCBE NY](https://www.ncbex.org/jurisdictions/NY)).
- **NextGen first in NY:** **July 2028**; NY will **not** accept NextGen transfer scores from exams before July 2028 ([NY BOLE, Jan 2026](https://www.nybarexam.org/Press/20260108%20Final%20Announcement%20-%20NextGen%20Bar%20Exam%20Transfer%20Scores.pdf)).
- **Filing deadlines (example):** July exam first deadline **March 31**; February exam **October 31** ([NCBE NY help](https://help2.ncbex.org/hc/en-us/articles/44041077389979-New-York-Admission-Eligibility-Deadlines-Fees-and-Scores)).

### Law school rhythm (NYC-heavy)

- **Fall finals:** mid-December.
- **Spring finals:** early–mid May.
- **MPRE:** Multiple dates; e.g. **November 2026** administrations ([NCBE](https://www.ncbex.org/)). Often taken **before** bar prep (1L/2L summer or early 3L).

### “Study leave” pattern

- **Big Law / many firms:** Paid or partially paid leave for **first bar attempt** (often tied to July exam).
- **Law students:** Summer after 2L often = internship; **post-grad May–July** = bar immersion — strongest analog to “retreat” demand.

## Medicine and health

### USMLE (MD)

- **Step 1 & Step 2 CK:** Prometric, **3‑month eligibility periods** (examinee picks window, then a day) ([USMLE BOI](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/applying-scheduling)).
- **Typical med-school cadence (LCME schools):**
  - **Step 1:** Often after second year — heavy prep **Apr–Jun**, test **May–Jul** (school-dependent; pass/fail era shifted timing but volume remains).
  - **Step 2 CK:** After core clerkships — often **summer/fall of third year** or early fourth year.
  - **Step 3:** During **PGY1**; less “retreat” oriented, more shift-work constrained.
- **No single national “USMLE week”** — demand is smoother than bar, but **May–August** still peaks for Step 1/2 cohorts.

### MCAT (premed)

- U.S. testing **January through September** ([AAMC register](https://students-residents.aamc.org/register-mcat-exam/register-mcat-exam)).
- **2026** included many dates Mar–Sep; **select NYC dates** on e.g. Jan 30, May 14, Jul 9, Aug 28 ([AAMC calendar](https://students-residents.aamc.org/register-mcat-exam/us-mcat-calendar-scheduling-deadlines-and-score-release-dates-0)).
- **Application cycle:** Many applicants test **Jan–May** before **June AMCAS** submission — spring is high-intensity prep.

### NCLEX (RN)

- **Computer-adaptive, year-round** at Pearson VUE — but volume is seasonal.
- **NCSBN data:** Largest quarterly **NCLEX-RN attempt volume** is **April–June** (~98–101k all-candidate attempts in 2024–2025 vs ~81–83k Jan–Mar) ([2024 PDF](https://www.ncsbn.org/public-files/2024_NCLEXExamStats_Final.pdf)).
- **Secondary peak:** **January** (December grads).
- **Quieter:** **September–December** (good for retreats if targeting nurses who deliberately delay testing).

### Other health (shorter notes)

- **PA (PANCE), pharmacy (NAPLEX), dental (INBDE):** Generally **rolling** with graduation-linked spikes **May–July** similar to NCLEX (verify per board when productizing a cohort).
- **Allied health / boards:** Often follow **May graduation** + **90-day** licensing prep.

## Other exams with clear seasonal patterns

| Exam | Pattern | Prep / retreat timing |
|------|---------|------------------------|
| **CPA** (Core AUD/FAR/REG) | Continuous testing | Rolling; busy before **30-month** clock deadlines |
| **CPA** (Discipline BAR/ISC/TCP) | **Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct** only ([AICPA](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/article/find-out-when-youll-get-your-cpa-exam-score)) | **2–4 weeks before** each month |
| **CFA L1** | **Feb, May, Aug, Nov** windows ([CFA](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/exam)) | **Dec–Jan, Mar–Apr, Jun–Jul, Sep–Oct** |
| **CFA L2/L3** | Fewer windows (often May/Aug/Nov) | Overlaps L1 shoulder seasons |
| **GRE / GMAT** | Mostly continuous at centers | Spikes with **application deadlines** (Dec, Jan for fall grad programs) |
| **LSAT** | Multiple dates per cycle | **Jun–Aug** and **Sep–Oct** prep common for fall applications |
| **Architect registration (ARE)** | Rolling at NCARB | Project-based; weaker retreat fit unless grouped |
| **Bar (already covered)** | Feb + Jul | May–Jul, Dec–Feb |

## Recommended retreat windows (NYC)

Target **multi-day, full-time focus** blocks **before** exam weeks. Examples use **typical** US calendars; pick exact weekends once you publish a yearly schedule.

| Window (approx.) | Primary audiences | Exams / milestones | Notes |
|------------------|-------------------|--------------------|--------|
| **Dec 8 – Dec 22** | Law students, undergrads, grad students | Fall finals | High willingness to pay for structure; holiday travel conflicts last week of Dec |
| **Jan 6 – Feb 15** | Feb bar takers; spring semester; NCLEX Jan cohort | Feb bar, MPRE follow-up | Include **weekend-only** options for working repeaters |
| **Mar 15 – Apr 25** | MCAT (May sittings); CPA Apr discipline; spring finals prep | MCAT, CPA, midterms | Good **premed + accounting** marketing |
| **May 15 – Jun 30** | Bar takers (July exam); new RN grads; Step 1/2 | July bar, NCLEX, USMLE | **Flagship bar-prep retreat**; highest demand |
| **Jul 8 – Jul 24** | Last-mile bar; repeaters | July bar (exam ~last week Jul) | Short **“final sprint”** sessions; exam ~Jul 28 |
| **Aug 1 – Aug 25** | MCAT Aug; CFA Aug; Step 2; retakers | MCAT, CFA, USMLE | Post-bar **repeater** niche in NYC |
| **Oct 1 – Nov 15** | CFA Nov; MPRE Nov; fall midterms/finals ramp | CFA, MPRE, finals | **Shoulder season** — less competition from bar; good for diversifying |
| **Nov 1 – Nov 30 (weekends)** | CPA Oct discipline retake prep; early Dec finals | CPA, finals | Lighter than May–Jul |

### Windows to de-prioritize

- **Thanksgiving week (US)** — low signup.
- **July 26 – Aug 2** — bar exam week (exhaustion, not retreat starts).
- **Late June** — some grads travel; still OK for **local** NYC cohorts.

### NYC angle

- **Law:** Large NYC grad pipeline → **May–July bar** is core.
- **Health:** NYC hospitals + nursing schools → **May–Jul NCLEX** + **year-round USMLE** at Prometric centers.
- **Premed:** MCAT dates explicitly list **New York, NY** on select days — market **spring** retreats hard.

## Gaps and assumptions

- **USMLE / Step timing** varies by school; no single official “national study leave” calendar — patterns are from typical LCME schedules, not USMLE-published dates.
- **NY bar study leave** is employer/policy-driven, not a single statute for all firms; culture is strong but not uniform.
- **NCLEX “peak”** inferred from NCSBN quarterly volumes, not published “exam dates.”
- **2027+ dates** for some exams may shift; re-check **NCBE**, **AAMC**, **USMLE**, **AICPA**, **CFA Institute** each fall when planning the next retreat calendar.
- **International / remote exams** omitted; Beside is **NYC in-person**.
